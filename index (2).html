About

I'm a mechanical engineering student in my third year, with a focus on SolidWorks that started from day one of my degree and has grown into hands-on project work — mechanisms, structures, and simulation-backed design.

I like taking a project past the modeling stage — checking it under load with FEA, or validating flow behaviour with CFD — rather than stopping at a finished render. It's how I've learned the tool: by building things that needed to actually hold up.
